A true carry-select adder instead precomputes the upper 12-bit sum twice in parallel (assuming cin=0 and cin=1, using the same efficient tool-inferred '+' adder synthesis that produces the best-known baseline), and only adds a single final 2:1 mux stage selecting based on the actual carry out of the lower 12-bit block.
